The research question

One broken link can undo everything before it.

A clinically correct referral can still fail if transport, receiving capacity, medicines, information or follow-up breaks. Researching reliability means following the system from the patient’s first contact through what happens next.

Closed-loop referral is a research lens: we study whether a referral progresses from decision to arrival, receiving care, return of information and follow-up. Sierra Leone Health Research does not operate the referral pathway.

Current research priorities

Study the system before prescribing the solution.

Our work separates policy, documented operating evidence, expert interpretation, lived experience and research hypotheses rather than treating them as interchangeable.
