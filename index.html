What we study

A health system is a chain, not a collection of buildings.

The research follows the full care journey: first contact, dependable primary care, referral, receiving capability, information return and follow-up. A technically correct policy can still fail if one of those links fails in practice.

Does first-contact care work when people need it?

Sierra Leone baseline

Start with the system that exists.

We separate three questions that are often blurred together: what policy says should happen, what evidence shows is happening, and what remains unknown.

That distinction matters. A national plan can be strong while real-world reliability still varies by facility, district, workforce availability, supply continuity, transport and patient circumstances.

Healthcare That Works

We study mechanisms. We do not copy countries.

Rwanda, Costa Rica and Cuba are comparison cases—not blueprints. We ask which mechanisms improve continuity and reliability, what conditions they depend on, and whether any part could plausibly transfer to Sierra Leone.
