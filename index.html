Research & Co-Design Phase

How can Sierra Leone build a more reliable health system?

A health system works only when the whole care journey works—from first contact to referral, treatment, return and follow-up.

We study what works, test what can realistically transfer to Sierra Leone, and make the assumptions, evidence and uncertainty visible in public.

The care journey

One broken link can undo everything before it.

A referral can be clinically correct and still fail if transport, receiving capacity, medicines, information or follow-up breaks. Reliability means the chain holds for the person who needs care.

HomeNeed appears
FrontlineFirst contact
PHUPrimary care
ReferralEscalation + transport
HospitalReceiving capability
ReturnInformation comes back
Follow-upContinuity closes the loop

Plain English: “Closed-loop referral” means the patient reaches the next level of care, the receiving team knows what happened, information returns, and follow-up continues.
