# Public Media Provenance Register

This register implements the portfolio wide public visual media protocol for Sierra Leone Health Research.

## Rule
Publicly available media may be used as an interim live website asset when reuse or embedding rights are sufficiently clear. Each asset must have a source, creator, rights status, placement, and replacement plan. Public visibility alone is not treated as permission.

## Replacement status
All third party assets are interim unless specifically promoted to permanent status after rights, relevance, quality and brand review. Original commissioned Sierra Leone photography and video should progressively replace the oldest and least representative Sierra Leone imagery first. Comparative country images may remain where their provenance is strong and they accurately illustrate the research case.
