Thematic systems tracer

Maternal health stress-tests many mechanisms at once.

Maternal health is not the institute's organizing thesis. It is a high-value tracer because safe care can depend on community identification, routine primary care, risk recognition, medicines, diagnostics, transport, referral, receiving capability and follow-up working together.

Sierra Leone, 2010 · Robert Yates/DFID · CC BY 2.0 · historical reference image

Research role

Use one care pathway to expose multiple system dependencies.

The tracer helps test whether a community-oriented primary-care model can remain dependable when routine care must connect to higher-acuity capability. It sits inside the wider transferability programme alongside workforce, PHU reliability, financing, supplies, referral and governance research.

StageWhat should happenSystem question
1. Pregnancy identifiedPregnancy enters a continuity pathway early enough for routine care and risk assessment.Does community responsibility make early identification more reliable?
2. Antenatal continuityMonitoring, prevention, screening and counselling occur reliably.Do staffing, supplies and follow-up systems support continuity?
3. Risk recognitionFront-line staff identify higher-risk cases and escalate before an emergency.Are skills, diagnostics and escalation protocols connected to real receiving capacity?
4. Delivery / routine referralThe delivery setting matches clinical need and the receiving site is known.Can the patient reach the right level at the right time?
5. Emergency escalationDanger signs trigger rapid action, transport and capable receiving care.What fails when transport, blood, theatre, staff, medicines or acceptance are unavailable?
6. Postnatal follow-upMother and newborn return to a responsible team with information and a follow-up plan.Does responsibility return to the community and primary-care layer?

What the tracer can reveal

Not just whether a referral exists — whether the system can support it.

A technically correct guideline or referral form can still fail when workforce, supplies, transport, receiving capability, information or financing break.

That makes maternal health useful for testing the enabling conditions behind the wider community-primary-care model rather than turning maternal care into the institute's sole mission.
