Thematic systems tracer

Maternal health shows whether the system works end to end.

It is a tracer—not the institute’s entire mission. One pregnancy can expose weaknesses in community contact, primary care, risk recognition, transport, referral, emergency capability and follow-up.

Six stages

Follow the journey, then find the break.

1 · Pregnancy identifiedEarly contact and continuity begin.
2 · Antenatal careMonitoring, prevention and screening continue.
3 · Risk recognisedHigher-risk cases are identified early.
4 · Right place for deliveryThe patient reaches the appropriate level of care.
5 · Emergency escalationTransport and receiving capability work when danger appears.
6 · Postnatal follow-upResponsibility and information return to continuing care.

What the tracer tests

A referral form is not a functioning referral system.

The pathway can fail because staff, medicines, diagnostics, transport, blood, theatre capacity, information or financing are missing at the moment they are needed.

The research asks where those failures occur, how often they occur and which enabling conditions would make the whole pathway more dependable.

Research boundary

Study the pathway. Do not pretend to operate it.

Sierra Leone Health Research measures and analyses the system. It does not provide maternal care, dispatch ambulances or run referral services.
