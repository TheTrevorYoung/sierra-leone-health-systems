Sierra Leone diagnosis

Sierra Leone already has a community health system. Why is it not reliable enough?

CHWs, PHUs, district structures and referral pathways already exist. The research focuses on the gap between design and dependable performance.

Kailahun Government Hospital, Laura Lartigue / USAID, public domain
Design

What exists

Community health, primary care, referral, workforce and national reform structures.

Reality

What must be tested

Staffing, supplies, facility capability, transport, information and affordability.

Research gap

Why reliability varies

Which conditions explain the difference between policy and performance?

What we diagnose

Six conditions that can make or break primary care.

Workforce

Staffing, skills, supervision and retention.

PHU capability

Medicines, diagnostics, equipment, power and water.

Supply chain

Procurement, storage and last mile reliability.

Financing

Household cost and sustainable operating funds.

Referral

Transport, receiving capacity and follow up.

Governance and data

Measurement, correction and accountability.

The central diagnosis

The problem may be implementation, not the absence of a model.

A sound policy can still fail when staffing, supplies, transport, financing or accountability break down.
