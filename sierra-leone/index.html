Sierra Leone Baseline

Understand the system that exists before drawing conclusions about it.

Our baseline tracks Sierra Leone’s CHW, PHU, hospital, referral, workforce, financing and reform architecture—what policy intends, what current evidence shows, and what remains unknown.

Policy / official plan

What policy says

National strategy and reform documents describe stronger primary health care, community delivery, workforce professionalisation, improved financing, digital systems and progress toward universal health coverage.

Implementation evidence

What evidence must test

Policy adoption does not prove medicines are in stock, referrals close, staff are present, information returns or households can afford care. Those are implementation questions.

Unknown

What remains to be learned

Catchment-level reliability, real referral completion, staff workload, supply continuity, patient costs, transport failures and many local operating conditions require stronger evidence.

System map

From community to referral care

CommunityHouseholds, community health workers, prevention, early identification
PHU / first-contact careEssential primary services, continuity, medicines, diagnostics, routine escalation
Higher-capacity supportDiagnostics, supervision, stabilization and services beyond first-contact capability

Current reform context

Track national direction without confusing plans with results.

The 2026–2030 planning cycle emphasizes primary health care, community service delivery, workforce, infrastructure, supply chains, financing, digital health and UHC. Sierra Leone Health Research studies how those priorities translate into operating reality and where evidence remains incomplete.
