# Sierra Leone Health Research — Design Reference

## Primary reference
**The Ocean Cleanup — https://theoceancleanup.com/**

The reference is used for design principles, not copied as a brand or mission template.

### What to borrow
- Large, purposeful photography that makes a technical subject tangible.
- Clear hierarchy: problem → research → evidence → current work → knowledge.
- Strong visual transitions between sections rather than long uninterrupted text.
- Research presented publicly through accessible explanation, field imagery, diagrams, data and source links.
- A knowledge architecture in which research, evidence and publications are easy to find.
- Media that shows the real environment in which the system operates.
- Short copy blocks paired with strong visual storytelling.

### What not to borrow
- Operational cleanup/deployment framing.
- Donation-first calls to action.
- Language implying Sierra Leone Health Research provides healthcare or runs facilities.
- Advocacy language presented as research findings.
- Any design element that reduces evidence labeling, uncertainty or methodological transparency.

## Sierra Leone Health Research adaptation
The public site should feel like a living, internationally credible research institute rather than a hospital website, NGO service-delivery site or text-heavy academic department.

Core visual sequence:
1. Sierra Leone health-system reality.
2. The research question.
3. Field context and care journey.
4. Current studies / tracers.
5. Evidence and methods.
6. Comparative health systems.
7. Findings, publications and updates as they become available.
8. Research collaboration / expert review.

## Media rule
Use rights-cleared public photography and video now. Prefer Sierra Leone-specific visuals. Every public asset is tracked in `MEDIA_PROVENANCE.md` and doubles as a creative brief for later commissioned local photography/video.
