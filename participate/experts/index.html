Expert Review Network

Review questions, not reputations.

Sierra Leone Health Research is building a question-specific review network. Reviewers are matched to defined research questions within their actual expertise.

Credential verificationRelevant professional identity and expertise are checked before technical review is represented publicly.
Conflict disclosureReviewers disclose material institutional, financial or professional conflicts relevant to the question.
Question-specific scopeReview of one research question does not imply endorsement of the whole organization.
Disagreement retainedMaterial expert disagreement is recorded rather than flattened into false consensus.
Permission before namingNo one is listed as an advisor, reviewer, partner or contributor without permission and accurate status.
Evidence remains decisiveExpert opinion informs interpretation; it does not become empirical proof simply because an expert said it.

Current status

Network formation: open concept, not a decorative roster.

Named reviewer profiles should appear only after permission, credential verification, role clarity and an actual review relationship exist. The value of the network is the quality of question-specific scrutiny, not the number of names displayed.
