What the network is for

Better research now. Better implementation knowledge over time.

The immediate value is stronger comparative evidence, sharper Sierra Leone diagnosis and more disciplined transferability judgments. Over time, the network also helps reveal what workforce, governance, financing and operating capability responsible real-world testing would require.

That long-term value does not make the research institute a healthcare operator.
