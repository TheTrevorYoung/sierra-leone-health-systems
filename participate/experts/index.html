Expert review network

Review a question, not the institution.

Experts are invited to scrutinize defined claims and methods in their actual area of knowledge. The purpose is independent challenge, not a decorative advisory board.

Priority review areas

Where outside scrutiny is most useful.

Community health

CHW systems, community participation, supervision and primary care integration.

Primary care operations

PHUs, workforce, medicines, diagnostics, referral and receiving capability.

Health systems methods

Implementation science, comparative methods, transferability and causal claims.

Financing and economics

Affordability, incentives, recurrent costs and financial sustainability.

Data and measurement

Health information systems, indicators, evidence quality and uncertainty.

Governance and institutions

Accountability, regulation, district structures and implementation conditions.

Review standard

Focused, transparent and attributable.

  1. Defined scope. Review one question, claim or assumption.
  2. Relevant expertise. Expertise matters only where it is relevant to the question.
  3. Conflicts disclosed. Material interests should be visible.
  4. Disagreement retained. We do not manufacture consensus.
  5. Permission before naming. No public affiliation is implied without consent.

Current phase

Initial reviews are small and voluntary.

Sierra Leone Health Research is currently unfunded. Early expert reviews are generally one asynchronous assignment of roughly 60 to 90 minutes. There is no recurring meeting requirement and no expectation of an ongoing role.

When a review materially contributes to a research output, acknowledgement or authorship is handled according to the actual contribution and accepted scholarly standards.

Current access

Invitation led while the network is being tested.

We are beginning with a small number of Sierra Leone based and diaspora researchers, practitioners and methods experts. A broader public expert interest channel will be added after the institutional email system is active and the first review workflow has been tested.
