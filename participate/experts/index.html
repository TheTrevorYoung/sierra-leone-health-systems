Expert Review Network

Review questions, not reputations.

The project is building a question-specific review network rather than a decorative advisory board. Reviewers are matched to defined questions within their actual expertise.

Credential verificationRelevant professional identity and expertise must be checked before technical review is represented publicly.
Conflict disclosureReviewers disclose material institutional, financial or professional conflicts relevant to the question.
Question-specific scopeReview of one component does not imply endorsement of the whole project.
Disagreement retainedMaterial expert disagreement is recorded rather than flattened into false consensus.
Permission before namingNo one is listed as an advisor, reviewer, partner or contributor without permission and accurate status.
Clinical standards are not crowdsourcedPublic preference never overrides professional, legal or safety requirements.

Named reviewer profiles should appear only after permission, credential verification, role clarity and an actual review relationship exist. The value of the network will be the quality of question-specific challenge—not the number of names displayed.
