Build the Model With Us

Good health-system design needs evidence, professional expertise and lived experience. They are not the same thing, and each has a different role.

Community experience

Share what the care journey feels like

Describe access barriers, continuity problems, transport, costs, communication and what happens after referral. Experience helps identify design questions; it does not establish clinical standards by vote.

Frontline practice

Explain operational reality

Health workers can identify workload, supervision, supply, documentation, referral and workflow problems that policy documents may miss.

Evidence

Submit research or source challenges

Researchers and institutions can submit relevant publications, data or corrections, including evidence that conflicts with the current model.

Expert review

Professional review network

Qualified reviewers can express interest in reviewing defined technical questions within their expertise.

Safety boundary

This is not a medical advice or emergency channel.

  • Do not submit emergency medical requests.
  • Do not request diagnosis or treatment.
  • Do not post another person’s identifiable health information.
  • No launch form should solicit detailed identifiable medical histories.

Launch form status

Forms are intentionally limited in the first build.

The static site includes the information architecture and safe contribution routes, but live submission endpoints will only be connected after privacy, security and data-retention review. Until then, no sensitive health data is collected through this site.
