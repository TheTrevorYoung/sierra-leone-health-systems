Contribute to the Research

Challenge the evidence. Improve the questions.

Sierra Leone Health Research welcomes relevant sources, factual corrections, research questions, operational context and qualified expert review.

What contribution means

Evidence and scrutiny—not endorsement.

A useful contribution can strengthen a source, identify a gap, challenge an interpretation or help us ask a better question.

Research-only boundary

This is not patient intake, medical advice, case management or a route to enroll in a healthcare service.

Safety boundary

Do not submit personal medical requests or sensitive records.

  • Do not submit emergency medical requests.
  • Do not request diagnosis or treatment.
  • Do not post another person’s identifiable health information.
  • Do not upload medical records through public GitHub issues.

Human-participant research

Formal research requires formal safeguards.

If Sierra Leone Health Research systematically collects health-related information from people to produce generalizable findings, an ethics determination and any applicable approvals must come before collection. Ordinary public comments are not presented as representative research findings.

SLSierra LeoneHealth Research

Independent health-systems research and analysis. We do not provide medical care, operate healthcare facilities, manage referrals, enroll patients or represent the Government of Sierra Leone.
