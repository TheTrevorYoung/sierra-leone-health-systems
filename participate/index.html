Participatory health systems research

Contribute to a defined research question.

We are building a small research network around specific evidence reviews, methodological questions and Sierra Leone implementation problems.

Current phase

Small assignments before formal membership.

Sierra Leone Health Research is currently unfunded. Initial contributions are voluntary, limited in scope and usually asynchronous. A prospective contributor starts with one defined review or research task. Ongoing involvement is considered only after both sides have worked together.

We do not recruit unpaid full time roles and we do not promise future employment or compensation.

Ways to contribute

Three participation paths.

Research contributor

Take on a micro research assignment

Review evidence, synthesize a defined question, compare a health system, or test a Sierra Leone implementation claim.

Expert review

Challenge a technical question

Provide focused scrutiny in your actual field without joining a standing advisory board.

Student and early career

Build research experience

Complete a clearly scoped assignment that can become part of a documented research record when the work meets the required standard.

Current opportunities

Two open research review tracks.

Sierra Leone

CHW policy versus implementation

Identify what Sierra Leone's Community Health Worker policy clearly establishes, what implementation evidence supports, where problems are documented, and what remains unknown.

Typical first review: about 60 to 90 minutes.

Methods

Participatory research structure critique

Test whether the contributor model gives researchers, practitioners and communities meaningful influence while protecting research quality, fair credit and appropriate safeguards.

Typical first review: about 60 to 90 minutes.

Current recruitment is invitation led.

Public contributor contact details will be added once the institutional email channel is active. Evidence challenges and corrections can still be submitted through the public research record.

Publication and credit

Contribution is recorded. Authorship is earned.

Meaningful contributors may be acknowledged publicly with permission. Authorship is considered only when a person's contribution meets accepted scholarly standards. A contribution does not guarantee authorship, publication, academic credit or employment.

Disagreement is not a problem. Strong review should be able to change the research.

How it works

One assignment at a time.

  1. Match. A person is approached for a question relevant to their knowledge.
  2. Review. They complete one small evidence or methods assignment.
  3. Assess. We check the work against the source record and research standard.
  4. Credit. Appropriate acknowledgement or authorship is discussed when warranted.
  5. Continue or stop. Further involvement is optional, not automatic.

Research boundary

Early participation is desk based.

Do not collect identifiable patient information, conduct health surveys, recruit human participants or undertake clinical activity as part of these early assignments. Formal human participant research requires the appropriate ethics and governance process.
