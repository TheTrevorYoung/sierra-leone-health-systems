How collaboration works

Different contributions require different standards.

Published evidence, frontline operational knowledge, policy context and specialist review are all useful, but they are not interchangeable. We label the source and evidentiary status of each contribution.

Research boundary

This is not a healthcare access, medical advice or emergency channel. Sierra Leone Health Research does not provide care or collect identifiable patient histories through the public website.

Lived experience · research protocol required

Patient and community experience matters—but it must be studied responsibly.

Experiences involving access, cost, transport, referral and follow-up can reveal system failures. We will not solicit identifiable medical histories through an informal public form.

Safety + ethics

Research access is not medical access.

  • Do not submit emergency medical requests.
  • Do not request diagnosis or treatment.
  • Do not post another person's identifiable health information.
  • Do not use public GitHub issues for confidential or sensitive information.
