Build the Model With Us

Good health-system design needs evidence, professional expertise and lived experience. They are not the same thing, and each has a different role.

Open now

A public, non-sensitive contribution route is available.

Evidence, source corrections, professional review interest and institutional or policy input can be shared now through the project’s public GitHub issue route.

Public route

GitHub issues are visible to everyone. Do not include personal medical information, emergency requests, private correspondence or anything you expect to remain confidential.

Share non-sensitive input ↗

A free GitHub account is required.

Community experience

Listening route in design

Community experience should help identify access, continuity, transport, cost and communication problems. Detailed personal medical histories are not being collected through the public site while a safer private process is designed.

Frontline practice

Explain operational reality

Health workers can identify workload, supervision, supply, documentation, referral and workflow problems that policy documents may miss. Non-sensitive operational input can use the public route above.

Evidence

Submit research or source challenges

Researchers can share relevant publications, data, corrections or evidence that conflicts with the current model through the public route above.

Institutional / policy

Add system context

Institutions and policy professionals can flag current plans, implementation realities, regulatory dependencies or public sources that the model should consider.

Corrections

Challenge a source or claim

Factual corrections and source challenges are welcome. Material corrections are recorded rather than silently rewritten.

Expert review network — forming

Review defined technical questions

Qualified professionals can review questions within their expertise. Credentials, conflicts and the scope of each review are checked before public attribution.

Safety boundary

This is not a medical advice or emergency channel.

  • Do not submit emergency medical requests.
  • Do not request diagnosis or treatment.
  • Do not post another person’s identifiable health information.
  • Do not use the public GitHub route for confidential information.

What is not open yet

Community health histories need a safer channel.

A structured route for personal experience will open only after privacy, security, consent and data-retention controls are defined. Until then, the site does not solicit detailed identifiable medical histories or sensitive health records.
