Build the Model With Us

Good health-system design needs lived experience, frontline practice, research evidence and professional expertise. Each has a different role.

Current participation architecture

Use the right route for the right kind of input.

GitHub is useful for transparent research corrections and technical challenges. It is not an appropriate principal gateway for community members, most frontline workers or institutional contributors.

What is open now

Public evidence submissions, source corrections and technical/model challenges can use the GitHub route. Community and frontline channels are being designed separately so they can be lower-friction and appropriately governed.

Community experience · route in design

Where does the care journey break?

Community experience can identify barriers involving access, transport, cost, communication, referral and follow-up. The future route should not require technical tools or developer accounts.

Detailed personal medical histories are not being collected through the public site.

Frontline practice · route in design

What does policy miss about daily operations?

Nurses, CHWs, midwives, facility staff and managers can surface workload, staffing, stock, documentation, transport, referral and workflow realities that formal plans may not show.

The planned route should be quick, mobile-friendly and usable without GitHub.

Safety boundary

This is not a medical advice or emergency channel.

  • Do not submit emergency medical requests.
  • Do not request diagnosis or treatment.
  • Do not post another person’s identifiable health information.
  • Do not use public GitHub issues for confidential or sensitive information.

Why the routes are staged

Lower friction cannot come at the expense of privacy or governance.

A future community or frontline intake route should be mobile-friendly and simple, but it must also define consent, privacy, data retention, safeguarding and how submissions affect the research record. Until those controls are ready, the project will not collect detailed identifiable medical histories or sensitive health records.
