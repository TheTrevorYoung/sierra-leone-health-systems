Future implementation pathway

Research → validate → pilot only if the gates close.

A future pilot is conditional. The existence of a promising model, funding interest or institutional support does not authorize clinical implementation.

Current

1. Research & co-design

Comparative research, Sierra Leone baseline, public input, expert review and Model 0.x.

Next

2. Technical blueprint & costing

Service package, workforce, facility, referral, data, economics and operational specifications.

Gate

3. Model 1.0 & validation

Resolve evidence conflicts, cost the design and perform institutional, legal and clinical validation.

Decision

4. GO / REVISE / NO-GO

Critical readiness domains constrain the decision. One red licensing or safety domain blocks launch.

Conditional

5. Defined-population pilot

Only with documented authorization, qualified governance, full-period funding and a responsible wind-down plan.

Evidence

6. Measure, publish, improve

Independent evaluation, adverse-event learning, cost evidence, model change and replication decision.

Hard rule

NO IMPLIED PERMISSION

Silence, informal support, a meeting, a letter of interest, historical practice, donor participation or the existence of a government facility does not automatically authorize the project to provide a regulated service.

Readiness domains

The weakest critical domain controls the launch decision.
