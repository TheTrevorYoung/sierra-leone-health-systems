Research Agenda

What we are studying next.

The agenda is organized around evidence gaps in Sierra Leone’s health system—not a timeline toward operating a clinical service.

Now

1. Sierra Leone baseline

Strengthen the evidence on PHCs/PHUs, CHWs, hospitals, workforce, financing, medicines, diagnostics, transport, referral, data and quality.

Now

2. Comparative systems

Standardize Cuba, Rwanda and Costa Rica comparisons and add other systems only when they answer a defined question.

Priority

3. Referral reliability

Study referral initiation, departure, transport, arrival, receiving care, return of information and follow-up.

Priority

4. Maternal health tracer

Use pregnancy-to-postnatal care to test how multiple parts of the system connect under routine and emergency conditions.

Next

5. System mechanics

Deepen work on workforce, medicines, diagnostics, financing, emergency transport, records and policy-to-implementation gaps.

Ongoing

6. Publish, review, correct

Release research with visible sources, expert review where needed, limitations, corrections and version history.

Permanent boundary

RESEARCH IS THE MISSION

Sierra Leone Health Research does not progress organizationally toward operating clinics, hospitals, referral services or a patient-facing pilot. Any future healthcare-delivery venture would be institutionally separate from this research entity.
