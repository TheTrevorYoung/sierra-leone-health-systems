Research translation

Research can inform action without becoming the operator.

Sierra Leone Health Research studies health-system problems, tests hypotheses and publishes evidence. Decisions to adopt, fund, regulate or operate services belong to the institutions legally responsible for those decisions.

01

Define the question

Start with a specific system problem, population, pathway or reliability failure.

02

Assemble evidence

Separate policy, published research, implementation evidence, lived experience and unknowns.

03

Test the hypothesis

Use comparative research and Sierra Leone-specific evidence to challenge the proposed mechanism.

04

Publish findings

State methods, limitations, uncertainty and what the evidence does—and does not—support.

05

Translate for decision-makers

Where warranted, explain policy or implementation implications without implying authority to adopt them.

06

Keep measuring

New evidence can confirm, narrow or overturn earlier conclusions.

Institutional boundary

NO IMPLIED AUTHORITY

A research finding, stakeholder meeting, funding discussion or institutional interest does not authorize Sierra Leone Health Research to provide regulated health services or speak on behalf of government.

What success looks like

Useful evidence that others can scrutinize and use.

The institute's contribution is better knowledge: clearer failure points, better measurements, stronger comparisons, transparent uncertainty and practical implications grounded in Sierra Leone's context.
