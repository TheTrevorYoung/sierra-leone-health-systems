Transferability Framework

Working somewhere else does not mean it will work in Sierra Leone.

Every idea has to pass an evidence and feasibility test.

Research only. This is not a clinical implementation plan.

The transfer test

Seven questions.

01

What is the mechanism?

Name the specific practice or function.

02

What is the evidence?

Separate findings from policy, opinion and hypothesis.

03

What makes it possible?

Workforce, financing, infrastructure, supplies and governance.

04

What exists in Sierra Leone?

Find the local equivalent and measure the gap.

05

What must change?

Adapt the mechanism rather than copy it.

06

Is it feasible?

Test cost, staffing, regulation, logistics and risk.

07

Does it earn a place?

Only supported ideas enter the Sierra Leone model.
