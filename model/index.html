Research framework 0.x

A set of hypotheses about what makes a health system reliable.

The framework connects population responsibility, dependable first-contact care, referral completion, information return and follow-up. Each component is a research proposition to test—not an implementation plan.

Boundary: Sierra Leone Health Research is a research institute. This framework does not authorize, operate or propose a clinical service.

Plain-English summary

Responsibility should not disappear between facilities.

The central research question is whether the care journey can remain connected from first contact through escalation and back to follow-up—and which system conditions make that possible or impossible.

01

Defined population

Study whether clearly assigned population responsibility improves continuity, prevention and follow-up.

Research question: Does someone know which households or patients they are responsible for?

Working hypothesis
02

Front-line continuity

Study how community and first-contact teams maintain continuity across prevention, routine care and navigation.

Research question: What makes responsibility persist beyond a single visit?

Working hypothesis
03

Minimum Reliable PHU

Define reliability by capability—staff, medicines, diagnostics, power, water, information and escalation—rather than the presence of a building.

Research question: What minimum conditions must reliably work at first-contact care?

Evidence supports further study
04

Higher-capacity support

Study whether an intermediate capability layer can improve diagnostics, supervision and stabilization without defaulting every escalation to hospital care.

Research question: Which functions need more capability than a PHU but less than a hospital?

Under review
05

Closed-loop referral

Measure referral as a completed process: patient arrival, receiving capability, information exchange, return and follow-up.

Research question: Where does the referral loop actually break?

Evidence supports further study
06

Measurement + learning

Use safety, quality, access, delay, cost and continuity evidence to revise the framework openly.

Research question: What evidence would confirm, narrow or overturn each hypothesis?
