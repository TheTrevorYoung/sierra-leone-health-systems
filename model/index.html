Research Framework

Study the care journey as one connected system.

Sierra Leone Health Research uses an end-to-end framework to investigate where reliability holds, where it breaks, and what evidence is still missing.

Research lens: this is not a clinical delivery blueprint and Sierra Leone Health Research does not operate the services being studied.

Core question

Does responsibility survive the handoff?

A health system can have facilities, policies and trained workers and still fail a patient if information, transport, receiving capacity or follow-up breaks between them.

01

First contact

How easily can people reach a dependable first point of care, and who is responsible for continuity?

Research domain
02

Primary care reliability

Are workforce, medicines, diagnostics, utilities, records and basic capability available when needed?

Research domain
03

Escalation

When a patient needs higher-level care, is the need recognized promptly and is an appropriate referral made?

Research domain
04

Referral completion

Does the patient actually depart, find transport, arrive and reach a capable receiving service?

Research domain
05

Referral closure

What happened at the receiving facility, does information return, and is the next step known?

Research domain
06

Follow-up

Does care continue after referral or discharge, and can the system detect when continuity is lost?

Research domain

Closed-loop referral

A measurement question, not a service promise.

We distinguish a referral being issued from a referral being completed and closed. Research may examine decision-to-departure time, transport, arrival, receiving capability, treatment, disposition, return of information and follow-up.

Boundary: Sierra Leone Health Research studies these pathways. It does not manage patients or operate a referral network.

Research domain

Health financing

Study financial protection, public financing, UHC reform and the economics that shape access and system reliability.
