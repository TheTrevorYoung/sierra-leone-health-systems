The evolving Sierra Leone model

A defined population → accountable front-line team → reliable PHU-linked primary care → higher-capacity support → formal referral network → documented return and follow-up.

Status: Not a clinical service. Not approved for implementation.
01

Defined population

Responsibility begins with knowing which households a team owns—not waiting for whoever arrives at a facility.

Working hypothesis
02

Front-line team

A mixed-skill team owns prevention, early identification, continuity and navigation within lawful scope.

Working hypothesis
03

Minimum Reliable PHU

Primary care is judged by dependable capability: staff, medicines, diagnostics, power, water, information and escalation—not by the presence of a building.

Evidence supports principle
04

Primary-care hub

A higher-capacity support layer may extend diagnostics, supervision and selected services without making hospitals the default entry point.

Under review
05

Closed-loop referral

Referral success requires completion, receiving information and return to the responsible team.

Evidence supports principle
06
