Health Financing Research

Who pays for care—and how does financing affect reliability?

We study health financing as part of the health system: household costs, public financing, donor dependence, financial protection, UHC reform and the incentives that shape access.

Sierra Leone financing baseline

The financing problem is measurable.

The National Health Accounts figures summarized by WHO for 2023 show that households and donors each financed a large share of total health expenditure. These figures describe the national context; they are not a budget or business plan for Sierra Leone Health Research.

Research questions

Financing is more than a national spending total.

  • What costs prevent people from seeking or completing care?
  • Which services are financially protected in policy and in practice?
  • How do financing flows affect medicines, diagnostics and workforce reliability?
  • How dependent are essential functions on external support?
  • What changes as Sierra Leone’s UHC financing architecture develops?
