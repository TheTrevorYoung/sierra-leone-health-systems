Research questions

Four tests

  1. Access: Do costs stop people from completing care?
  2. Sustainability: Which recurrent costs threaten reliability?
  3. Affordability: Which mechanisms remain realistic at scale?
  4. Compatibility: How do findings fit national financing reforms?
