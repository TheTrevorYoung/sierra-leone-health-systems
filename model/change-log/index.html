September 2026 · Model architecture

Referral success redefined as closure

Previous position: referrals were discussed mainly as routing.

Decision: referral success now requires documented completion, information return and follow-up ownership.

Locked design rule
September 2026 · Financing

Cash budget separated from economic cost

Reason: donated or government-provided resources still have real replication cost.

Decision: solvency, recurrent sustainability, replication affordability and national compatibility are evaluated separately.

Locked design rule
September 2026 · Governance

NO IMPLIED PERMISSION

Reason: informal interest, a meeting or use of a public facility can be mistaken for clinical authorization.

Decision: every regulated service, professional role and facility requirement must be verified before a clinical launch.

Locked readiness rule
September 2026 · Public identity

Research website separated from future provider role

Decision: Sierra Leone Health Systems launches publicly as a research and co-design initiative, not as a clinic, hospital, insurer or approved government program.
