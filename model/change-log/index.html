Research updates

Material changes to research framing, methodology and interpretation should remain visible rather than being silently rewritten.

6 September 2026 · Institutional identity

Sierra Leone Health Research locked as a pure research entity

Previous position: the project combined research with public co-design of an evolving healthcare-delivery model and a possible future pilot pathway.

New position: Sierra Leone Health Research is permanently research-only. It conducts research, analysis, evidence synthesis, public explanation and expert review. It does not progress toward operating healthcare services under this entity.

Website effect: Model becomes Research Framework; Build the Model With Us becomes Contribute to the Research; Pilot Pathway becomes Research Agenda.

Locked strategic clarification
6 September 2026 · Referral research

Closed-loop referral retained as a research lens

Clarification: referral closure remains central because it tests whether a patient reaches receiving care, information returns and follow-up continues.

Boundary: Sierra Leone Health Research studies referral reliability; it does not operate or manage referrals.

Research framework
6 September 2026 · Maternal health

Maternal health retained as the first systems tracer

Clarification: pregnancy-to-postnatal care is used to stress-test multiple parts of the health system at once. It is not a Sierra Leone Health Research service program.

Research framework
September 2026 · Evidence governance

Policy remains separate from implementation evidence

Rule: legislation, strategy or official announcement establishes policy intent; it does not by itself prove operating implementation or outcomes.

Locked evidence rule
September 2026 · Comparative method

Country comparisons remain mechanism-first

Rule: Cuba, Rwanda, Costa Rica and other systems are studied to isolate mechanisms and operating conditions—not as blueprints to copy or countries to promote.

Locked comparative rule
September 2026 · Permission and claims

NO IMPLIED PERMISSION retained

Rule: meetings, informal support, historical practice, access to public information or institutional interest never become claims of partnership, approval or authority without direct evidence.

Locked claims rule
