Evidence Library

Make every important claim traceable.

The library supports three linked tasks: comparing community-oriented primary healthcare systems, diagnosing Sierra Leone's operating reality and testing whether a mechanism can transfer.

Research hypothesis

A proposition that still has to survive evidence and transferability testing.

How sources are used

Evidence must answer a specific question.

A source about Cuba cannot establish that a Cuban mechanism will work in Sierra Leone. A Sierra Leone policy cannot establish that the policy is reliably implemented. A historical facility survey cannot be presented as a current national census. Every source is interpreted within its scope.

Evidence discipline

The library should be able to weaken our preferred conclusion.

Contradictory evidence is not a problem to hide. It should narrow confidence, change the transferability judgment or change the Sierra Leone model when warranted.
