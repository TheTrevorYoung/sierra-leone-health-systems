Source governance

AI summaries are research aids, never evidence records by themselves.

Each material research page should retain the underlying source, publication date, evidence class, limitations, last-reviewed date and where the source is used. High-stakes claims should prioritize primary or authoritative sources and clearly distinguish inference from source language.
