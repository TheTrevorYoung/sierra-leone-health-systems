Methodology

Make uncertainty visible without being uncertain about the purpose.

The project distinguishes facts, policy, implementation evidence, expert interpretation, lived experience and hypotheses so that a compelling narrative never outruns what the evidence can support.

Method

Claim classesEvidenceParticipationExpert reviewVersioning

Claim classes

Major statements are classified as PUBLIC NOW, PUBLIC WITH QUALIFIER, INTERNAL ONLY or PROHIBITED UNTIL GATE. Clinical, licensing, partnership, financing or implementation claims cannot move into public language without documentary evidence.

Evidence

Primary and authoritative sources are preferred for high-stakes claims. Policy documents are not treated as proof of implementation. Sources carry dates and limitations, and material research pages include correction routes.

Participation

Lived experience helps identify problems and priorities. It does not determine clinical standards by vote. Inputs are classified as experience, evidence, expertise or preference.

Expert review

Reviewers are matched to questions, credentials are verified where relevant, conflicts are disclosed and disagreement is retained. Reviewing one question does not imply endorsement of the entire project.

Versioning

Material model changes are logged with the previous position, new evidence or input, the decision, the reason and affected components. Silent rewriting of material findings is avoided.
