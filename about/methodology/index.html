Methodology

Make uncertainty visible without being uncertain about the mission.

Sierra Leone Health Research separates documented facts, policy, implementation evidence, expert interpretation, lived experience and hypotheses so that conclusions do not outrun the evidence.

Research questions first

Major work begins with a defined question. Comparative research is used to understand mechanisms and conditions, not to search selectively for support for a preferred solution.

Evidence

Primary and authoritative sources are preferred for high-stakes claims. Policy documents establish policy intent; they are not treated as proof of operating implementation. Sources should carry dates, context and material limitations.

Claim labels

Important statements may be identified as documented fact, policy / official plan, implementation evidence, evidence-supported interpretation, expert view, lived experience, project hypothesis, under review, corrected or superseded.

Expert review

Reviewers are matched to questions within their actual expertise. Credentials and relevant conflicts are checked, and material disagreement is retained rather than converted into artificial consensus.

Ethics & privacy

Ordinary comments and corrections are not automatically research findings. Systematic collection of health-related information from human participants for generalizable research requires an ethics determination before collection and any applicable approvals. Sensitive health data is not solicited through ordinary public website forms.

Publication gates

Before publication, applicable work must pass source, interpretation, expert-review, ethics/privacy and conflict/independence checks. AI may support research operations but is never evidence and does not replace source verification or qualified review.

Corrections and versioning

Material research changes record what changed, why, which evidence caused the revision and what uncertainty remains. Research assets may be versioned; this versioning does not represent an evolving healthcare-delivery model.

Research-only boundary

Methods govern research, not clinical operations.

Sierra Leone Health Research does not provide medical care, operate healthcare facilities, manage referrals or enroll patients. Any research involving human participants is governed as research, not disguised as public engagement.
