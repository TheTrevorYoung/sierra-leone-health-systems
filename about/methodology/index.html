Methodology

Make uncertainty visible.

Separate what is documented from what is assumed, then make every transfer claim earn its place.

Method

ComparisonDiagnosisTransferabilityEvidenceReviewChanges

Compare systems consistently

Use the same questions for each country. Population responsibility, community access, primary care capability, workforce, continuity, financing, information and governance.

Separate policy from reality

Map what Sierra Leone already has, then distinguish official design from evidence about how it works in practice.

Test transferability

Define the mechanism, examine the evidence, identify its enabling conditions, compare those conditions with Sierra Leone and test feasibility.

Keep evidence classes distinct

Facts, policy, implementation evidence, expert interpretation, lived experience and hypotheses are not interchangeable. AI can assist research work, but it is not evidence.

Use expertise carefully

Match reviewers to defined questions, verify relevant expertise, disclose conflicts and retain meaningful disagreement.

Show material changes

When new evidence changes a conclusion, record what changed and why.
