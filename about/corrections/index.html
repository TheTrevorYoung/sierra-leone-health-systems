Corrections

Errors should leave a visible trail.

Material factual, interpretation or model changes are recorded rather than silently rewritten.

01

Minor factual correction

A limited error that does not change the overall interpretation.

02

Source / status update

A source, date or current-status statement changes as newer evidence becomes available.

03

Material interpretation change

New evidence changes the explanation, confidence or transferability conclusion.

04

Framework change

A correction or new evidence changes a transferability judgment or Sierra Leone model hypothesis.

05

Retraction / removal

Content is materially unreliable, unsafe, rights-infringing or otherwise should not remain public.

Correction record

What changed · why · affected research

Corrections should identify the previous position, new evidence or error, the decision and the pages or conclusions affected. A correction can strengthen, narrow or overturn a preferred hypothesis.
