Sierra Leone implementation study · 01

A PHU is reliable only when the essentials actually work.

We are testing the minimum combination of people, medicines, diagnostics, infrastructure, supervision and escalation capability needed for dependable first-contact care.

Sierra Leone, 2012 · USAID/P. Nshimirimana · Public domain · source

The question

Presence is not capability.

A facility can appear on a map and still fail at first contact. The research therefore measures what must be present, functioning and supported—not simply whether a building exists.

536facilities in the 2018 Service Delivery Indicators sample
5,055workers covered by absenteeism rosters
829health workers assessed with patient-case simulations

Historical baseline only. These figures help define what to measure; they are not presented as a current nationwide PHU census.

Capability test

Seven things have to work together.

PeopleRequired staff are present, skilled, supervised and working within a viable employment structure.
Medicines + suppliesEssential items are available when patients need them.
InfrastructureWater, sanitation and electricity meet the needs of intended services.
Equipment + diagnosticsEssential tools function and have the consumables they require.
Clinical capabilityThe team can recognise, manage and escalate within its service package.
Management + dataSupervision, measurement and problem correction are functioning.
ContinuityReferral, information return and follow-up connect the PHU to the rest of the system.

What the evidence supports

Build a current capability baseline.

The next step is to test verified facility and district evidence against this capability bundle. Missing evidence stays visible; it is not replaced by assumption.
