Research rule

We study mechanisms. We do not copy countries.

No model is treated as a blueprint. Political systems, workforce supply, financing, geography, institutions and public expectations differ. A useful comparison identifies mechanisms that may travel—and the conditions they depend on.

Current research shift

From literature evidence toward Sierra Leone implementation evidence.

The first implementation note tests the Minimum Reliable PHU concept against published Sierra Leone facility and workforce evidence. Future notes should add current referral pathways, frontline practice, facility operations and cost structures as verified evidence becomes available.
