Research agenda

Start with the failure question.

The work is organized around concrete system questions: what does dependable first-contact care require, why referrals fail, how information returns, how maternal care tests the full pathway, and which mechanisms from other systems may travel.

Research method

We study mechanisms. We do not copy countries.

A useful comparison asks why a mechanism works, what resources and institutions it depends on, and what would have to be true for it to work in Sierra Leone.

Political systems, geography, workforce supply, financing and public expectations differ. Those differences are part of the research—not inconvenient details to ignore.
