Research agenda

What makes community-oriented primary healthcare work — and what could transfer to Sierra Leone?

Our research moves from comparison to diagnosis to transferability. We do not begin with a preferred foreign model or assume the answer before the evidence is examined.

Koidu Hospital, 2006 · L. Lartigue/USAID · Public domain · historical reference image

Primary research question

Transferability is the test.

What features of effective community-oriented primary healthcare systems are transferable to Sierra Leone, and what institutional, workforce, financing, infrastructure and operational conditions are required for those features to work reliably at scale?

Six questions underneath it

From curiosity to a Sierra Leone-specific answer.

01

What makes effective systems work?

Identify mechanisms, enabling conditions, costs, weaknesses and tradeoffs across comparison countries.

02

What does Sierra Leone already have?

Map community health, CHWs, PHUs, district care, hospitals, financing, workforce, supplies, data and governance.

03

Why is reliability inconsistent?

Separate written architecture from implementation and identify the conditions that prevent intended performance.

04

What is genuinely transferable?

Test mechanisms rather than copying countries or labels.

05

What would Sierra Leone need?

Identify workforce, financing, infrastructure, supply, data, governance and operational requirements.

06

What should the Sierra Leone model become?

Build a versioned model from the evidence and change it when the evidence changes.

Research architecture

Comparison → diagnosis → transferability → model.

Each stage constrains the next. A promising mechanism from another country does not enter the Sierra Leone model until its evidence and enabling conditions have been examined against local reality.

Research standard

The work must be able to disprove our preferred idea.

If the evidence shows that a mechanism cannot transfer, costs too much, depends on conditions Sierra Leone cannot realistically build, or performs worse than an alternative, the model should change.
