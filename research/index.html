Research workflow

Every product follows the same basic sequence.

  1. Question. Define the claim or mechanism being tested.
  2. Evidence. Find and assess primary, institutional and peer reviewed sources.
  3. Sierra Leone relevance. Test the finding against local conditions and evidence.
  4. Review. Invite qualified contributors to challenge the interpretation where useful.
  5. Publish and revise. Show sources, limitations, changes and unresolved questions.

Contribute

Research tasks are small and specific.

Prospective contributors begin with a defined review or evidence assignment. Reliable contributions can lead to further work, acknowledgement, or authorship when accepted scholarly standards are met.
