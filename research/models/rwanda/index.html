Africa comparison

Rwanda

Rwanda connects community health workers, health posts, health centres and referral hospitals. We study what makes those links work.

Mechanisms worth studying

  • CHWs connected to formal primary care.
  • Health posts between communities and health centres.
  • Clear referral levels and escalation.
  • District level supervision and planning.

Major cautions

  • Governance and financing conditions differ from Sierra Leone.
  • Access does not automatically prove quality or continuity.
  • Supervision, payment and supply reliability still matter.

Sierra Leone transfer test

Could stronger integration improve what already exists?

CHWsCan supervision, scope and accountability be strengthened?
PHUsWhich existing facilities can provide stronger support without adding unnecessary layers?
ReferralCan escalation be clearer, measurable and connected to receiving capacity?
District managementWhich supervision, logistics and data functions need district ownership?

Transfer lesson

The useful idea may be integration.

Sierra Leone already has CHWs and PHUs. The question is how to make the links between them more dependable.
