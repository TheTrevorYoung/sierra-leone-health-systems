African comparator

Rwanda

Rwanda is useful because community health workers, health posts, health centres and referral hospitals are deliberately connected across levels of care. The research question is which mechanisms matter — and which administrative, workforce and financing conditions make them possible.

Why study it

An African comparison with explicit community-to-facility architecture.

Rwanda helps test whether community reach can be connected to tiered primary-care capability without treating every point of care as a hospital.

Research boundary

Strong architecture does not prove every quality, continuity or equity problem is solved. “Worth testing” is not the same as “copy this model.”

Mechanisms worth testing

  • Community health workers integrated into a broader delivery system.
  • Health posts positioned between community outreach and health centres.
  • Explicit referral levels and escalation architecture.
  • National planning that links workforce, facility readiness and health outcomes.

Enabling conditions + cautions

  • Governance, administrative execution and financing conditions differ from Sierra Leone.
  • Published access improvements do not prove uniform quality or continuity.
  • Workforce payment, supervision and supply reliability must be examined separately.
  • Private participation in health posts raises incentive, financing and regulation questions.

Sierra Leone transfer test

What would have to be true here?

CHW integrationCan Sierra Leone strengthen CHW supervision, scope, compensation and accountability inside PHU-linked teams?
Intermediate accessWhich existing PHU or CHC functions could provide closer, higher-capacity support without duplicating structures?
Referral architectureCan routine and emergency escalation be made explicit, measurable and connected to receiving capability?
District managementWhich supervision, logistics, data and accountability functions require district ownership?
FinancingWhich financing and incentive conditions are essential to make community and facility layers dependable?

Transfer lesson

The promising idea may be integration, not replication.

Sierra Leone already has CHWs and PHUs. The useful question is whether stronger supervision, clearer role definition, better facility capability and more dependable links between levels can improve the architecture that already exists.
