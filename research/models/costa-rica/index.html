Central America comparison

Costa Rica

Costa Rica uses community based, multiprofessional primary care teams with responsibility for defined populations.

Mechanisms worth studying

  • Defined geographic responsibility.
  • Multiprofessional primary care teams.
  • Prevention and clinical care in the same local system.
  • Higher level support for frontline teams.

Major cautions

  • Costa Rica has stronger fiscal and institutional capacity.
  • Its formal team mix may be too expensive to copy directly.
  • Management, data and supply systems may matter as much as team design.

Transfer lesson

The useful idea is the function, not the EBAIS label.

Sierra Leone has to test whether defined responsibility, team care and prevention can work through its own workforce and institutions.
