Integrated primary-care comparator

Costa Rica

Costa Rica is useful because multiprofessional primary-care teams combine prevention, public health and clinical care inside defined communities. The research asks which functions matter, what institutional and financing conditions support them, and what Sierra Leone could realistically adapt.

Why study it

Team-based community responsibility with integrated support.

Costa Rica offers a different route to community-oriented primary care than Cuba or Rwanda. That difference helps distinguish the mechanism from the country-specific form.

Research boundary

Formal team composition, social-insurance history and institutional capacity cannot be assumed transferable. The research isolates functions before considering adaptation.

Mechanisms worth testing

  • Multiprofessional teams combining preventive, public-health and curative functions.
  • Teams embedded in defined communities rather than disconnected facilities.
  • Higher-level support extending capacity beyond the basic front line.
  • Community participation and intersectoral action connecting health services to local needs.

Enabling conditions + cautions

  • Fiscal capacity, institutional history and social-insurance structure differ from Sierra Leone.
  • Formal team composition may be too workforce-intensive or expensive to transfer directly.
  • Management, data and supply systems around the team may matter as much as the team itself.
  • Financing architecture requires separate analysis before payment mechanisms are inferred.

Sierra Leone transfer test

What would have to be true here?

Defined responsibilityCan a Sierra Leone team be accountable for a defined population using existing CHW and PHU structures?
Multiprofessional mixWhat minimum skill mix could reproduce the function without reproducing the cost?
Support layerCould CHCs, district structures or another support arrangement extend diagnostics, supervision and specialist input?
Integrated preventionCan prevention and case finding become routine team responsibilities rather than campaign-only activity?
Management + dataWhat information and accountability systems would make geographic responsibility meaningful?
FinancingWhat financing arrangement could sustain the adapted functions in Sierra Leone's context?

Transfer lesson

Copy the function only if the conditions can travel.

The promising idea is not the name EBAIS. It is the combination of defined responsibility, team-based primary care, prevention, support and management. Sierra Leone must test whether those functions can be delivered through its own workforce and institutions.
