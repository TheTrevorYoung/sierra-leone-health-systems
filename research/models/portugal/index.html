Portugal

Portugal is useful because Family Health Units organize multidisciplinary primary-care teams around registered populations with team autonomy and performance accountability.

Status: Under reviewLast reviewed: 6 September 2026

Mechanisms worth studying

  • Defined registered populations.
  • GPs, nurses and other professionals working as teams.
  • Greater team autonomy than traditional primary-care units.
  • Performance measurement tied to access and quality.

Major cautions

  • Portugal has far greater fiscal and workforce capacity.
  • Its NHS institutions and payment systems cannot be assumed transferable.
  • The useful lesson may be team accountability, not the exact unit design.
