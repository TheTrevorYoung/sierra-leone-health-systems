Caribbean comparison

Cuba

Cuba sparked the inquiry. We study its community focused primary care mechanisms, then test whether they can work in Sierra Leone.

Mechanisms worth studying

  • Defined populations for local teams.
  • Prevention built into routine primary care.
  • Doctor and nurse teams linked to polyclinic support.
  • Referral and follow up treated as a continuum.

Major cautions

  • Cuba's state structure and financing are different.
  • Its physician heavy frontline may not be realistic for Sierra Leone.
  • Current shortages and workforce pressures matter when judging performance.

Sierra Leone transfer test

What would have to be true here?

ResponsibilityCould a named team be responsible for a defined catchment?
Team designWhat affordable mix of cadres could deliver the same function?
SupportWhich PHU, CHC or district functions could provide diagnostics and escalation?
ContinuityHow would information and follow up continue after referral?

Decision rule

Cuba can inspire the question and still fail the test.

If the mechanism depends on conditions Sierra Leone cannot realistically build, it should change or be rejected.
