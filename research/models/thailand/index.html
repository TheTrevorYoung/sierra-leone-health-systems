Asia comparison

Thailand

Thailand links village health volunteers, local health centres and district hospitals inside a long running primary care system.

Mechanisms worth studying

  • Village health volunteers embedded in communities.
  • Health centres linked to district hospitals.
  • Referral and return of information across levels.
  • Primary care supported by national financing.

Major cautions

  • Thailand has much stronger fiscal and institutional capacity.
  • Volunteer scale depends on training and supervision.
  • Its purchasing system and district hospital capacity cannot be copied directly.
