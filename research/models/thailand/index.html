Asia comparison

Thailand

Thailand is useful because community volunteers, local health centres and district hospitals are linked inside a long-running primary-care system reinforced by universal coverage.

Status: Under reviewLast reviewed: 6 September 2026

Mechanisms worth studying

  • Village health volunteers embedded in communities.
  • Health centres linked to district hospitals.
  • Referral and back-referral across levels.
  • Primary care reinforced by national purchasing and UHC policy.

Major cautions

  • Thailand's fiscal and institutional capacity is much stronger.
  • Volunteer scale depends on training, supervision and public infrastructure.
  • Its purchasing system and district-hospital capacity cannot be copied directly.
