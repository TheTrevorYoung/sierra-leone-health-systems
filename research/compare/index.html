Cross-system synthesis

Compare mechanisms, not countries.

Cuba, Rwanda and Costa Rica are useful for different reasons. Sierra Leone already has parts of similar architecture. The research asks which mechanisms appear useful, which conditions differ, and what remains uncertain.

Current synthesis

The recurring research theme is accountability, not institutional imitation.

Across these comparison systems, recurring mechanisms include a defined population, capable first-contact care, higher-capacity support and connected referral. Whether those mechanisms work similarly in Sierra Leone depends on workforce, medicines, information, financing, governance and real operating conditions.
