Cross-model synthesis

Compare mechanisms, not countries.

Cuba, Rwanda and Costa Rica are useful for different reasons. Sierra Leone already has pieces of the same architecture. The comparison asks which mechanisms travel, which conditions do not, and what must be tested locally.

Current synthesis

The common mechanism is accountability, not institutional imitation.

Across the three comparison systems, the strongest recurring design idea is a defined population linked to a capable first-contact team, backed by a higher-capacity layer and a referral pathway. Sierra Leone already has community and PHU structures that could support that logic if reliability, workforce, medicines, information and financing can be made real.
