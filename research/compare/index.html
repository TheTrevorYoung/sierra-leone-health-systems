Cross-system synthesis

Compare mechanisms — and the conditions that make them possible.

Cuba, Rwanda and Costa Rica are useful for different reasons. Sierra Leone already has parts of similar community-primary-care architecture. The research asks what mechanism appears useful, what enables it, what the cautions are and what local adaptation would be required.

Current synthesis

The recurring ideas are hypotheses, not conclusions.

Defined population responsibility, supported front-line teams, capable primary care and connected referral appear repeatedly across the comparison systems. The next step is not to declare them “best practice”; it is to test their evidence, enabling conditions, Sierra Leone equivalents, gaps, costs and tradeoffs.
